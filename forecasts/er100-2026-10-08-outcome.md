## 1. What was released

Source: Life Biosciences press release, 8 October 2026, ["Life Biosciences Announces First-in-Human Data from Ongoing Phase 1 Trial Evaluating ER-100 in Optic Neuropathies"](https://www.lifebiosciences.com/life-biosciences-announces-first-in-human-data-from-ongoing-phase-1-trial-evaluating-er-100-in-optic-neuropathies/). The conference slides were not available when this file was written.

- **Participants:** three, all with open-angle glaucoma (OAG). No NAION participant reported.
- **Dose:** Dose Level 1, 2×10¹¹ vg, single intravitreal injection; oral doxycycline for 8 weeks to switch on OSK expression; an **oral prednisolone taper** to reduce the risk of inflammation from the AAV2 immune response.
- **Follow-up:** to Day 56.
- **Safety:** well tolerated in all three; no dose-limiting toxicities, no adverse events of special interest, no serious adverse events. All reported adverse events attributed to the procedure, the steroid or pre-existing conditions, not to ER-100. Intraocular inflammation and eye-pressure changes are not described.
- **Visual function:** Humphrey visual field improvements in 2 of 3 participants; in each of those two, at least 28% of visual-field test points improved by more than 4 dB. Mean deviation (MD) is not reported.
- **Not reported:** visual acuity, contrast sensitivity, pERG, OCT nerve-fibre or ganglion-cell thickness, any comparison with the untreated fellow eye, any statistical test.
- **Next step:** the Data Safety Monitoring Board recommended escalation to Dose Level 2 (6×10¹¹ vg) in OAG.

## 3. Reading under the pre-committed rules

**Outcome: C** — safety as in A, plus a visual improvement that does not meet the pre-registered criteria for B.

Why not B. The pre-commitment required, for B, improvement beyond the noise thresholds, consistent across at least one functional **and** one structural measure, in most participants, and larger than in the fellow eye. The release reports:

- one measure only (visual field), and a subjective one — it depends on the participant's responses, which the pre-commitment weighted less than objective tests (pERG, OCT);
- point-wise changes (>4 dB at ≥28% of points), not the mean deviation for which the 2 dB noise threshold was fixed; point-wise test–retest variability in glaucomatous visual fields is large, and repeated testing produces learning effects;
- no structural measure, no fellow-eye comparison, no masking, three participants, 56 days.

None of this means the improvement is not real; it means the evidence is not of the kind the pre-commitment said would count as a functional signal.

**Updates, as fixed in advance:**

## 4. What to watch next

- Dose Level 2 safety, and whether inflammation appears at the higher dose.
- Objective structural and electrophysiological measures (OCT, pERG) and fellow-eye comparisons.
- The NAION cohort, which has not started.
- Durability after doxycycline is stopped (follow-up to Day 112 and beyond).
